ABOUT KYVOR DESIGNS

Founded by Kartikay Bansal in Kanpur, KYVOR DESIGNS offers high-quality streetwear with ultra-durable DTF prints and heavy 240 GSM bio-washed cotton fabric.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which sizes are available across products?
We offer sizes 22, 24, 26 (Kids) and S, M, L, XL, XXL (Adult Oversized Fits). Specific ready catalog tees like the Deer Tee are offered in selected sizes like M and L.
How does custom print ordering work?
Fill out the Custom Print form with your placement, details, size & color. Click "Submit On WhatsApp" to directly connect with our design team.
How does payment work for catalog orders?
Fill in your shipping details, click "Proceed To Pay First", scan the QR code (`images/qr.jpeg`), enter the 12-digit UTR number, and confirm on WhatsApp!
